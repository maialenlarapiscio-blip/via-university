# 11 Outreach playbook (Twenty CRM)

Source: "Maia's Outreach Playbook — Twenty CRM", Magnus Damiri, 6 Nov 2026. Questions or ideas about the setup go to HC (Slack or hc@getregulars.com) with the company, the button and a screenshot.

## The goal
One goal behind every email and call: **a booked demo**, about 30 minutes online with Maia and HC. Sell the meeting, not the product.

## One email, step by step
1. Call Center → Call mode. The week's list is on the left.
2. Read the brief. **Stop** if it says ⚠️ Muligvis allerede kunde, 🔗 Chain called before, or shows earlier contact under Tidligere kontakt (see the rules).
3. 2–3 minutes on their website, Instagram and booking button: what they sell, who owns it, which booking or loyalty system they use. Write **one fact** as a note.
4. Check the contact. Owner or manager by name beats info@. Add the right person if needed.
5. Language: Danish venues get Danish unless the owner writes English.
6. Type the note in Note for this call, press Enter.
7. Other outcome → Send email → Save ("Saved ✓").
8. Twenty makes the Gmail draft with a demo card of their brand, logs it, and books a follow-up call in 7 days.
9. Gmail → Drafts: check name, card image and signature, then **rewrite the first line with the fact from step 3**.
10. Press Send. Drafts are never sent automatically.
If no draft appears, a Slack message has the text: send it from Gmail the same day.

## The email
Twenty writes it: subject "✨ [Brand] × Regulars", short pitch, demo card (stamp card for cafés, cashback for restaurants and bakeries, membership for bars and hotels), a question at the end. Maia only writes the **first line**, with one real fact. Keep the rest as it is: short, one question, "Best regards," and signature. No attachments or extra links.

Good first lines:
1. "Saw you just opened a second shop on Jægersborggade, congratulations."
2. "Your brunch menu is all over my Instagram this week."
3. "I noticed you book tables through Superb, so you know the guests who book, but not the ones who walk in."

## The follow-up call (7 days later)
Goal: a 20 minute demo, ideally within 72 hours.
1. **Open:** "Hi [name], it's Maia from Regulars. I sent you an email last week with a card we made for [Brand]. Did you get a chance to see it?"
2. **Problem:** "Most [restaurants/cafés] I talk to don't really know who their regulars are. How do you get guests to come back today?"
3. **Dig:** "How do you keep track of when they come back?" Booking systems only see the person who books, not walk-ins or the rest of the table.
4. **Name drop:** "That's exactly what we did for Aamanns."
5. **Close with two options:** "Are you more flexible at the end of this week or early next week?" Never "sometime next week".
6. **No to the meeting = yes to an email:** "That's fair. Can I send you a short email, so you have my contact?" → Send information.

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I'm busy right now." | "That's fair. Can I just send an email, so you have my contact?" |
| "We can't afford it." | "I understand. How do you keep track of guests today?" Then offer the email. |
| "We already have an app." | Don't fight on loyalty. "How do you handle your company deals and corporate perks?" |
| "Just send an email." | "Happy to. Who should it go to, and is it guest data or return visits you care about most?" |

Gatekeeper: get the right person's name and email, spell it back, log Gatekeeper.

## Buttons
| Button | When | Twenty does |
|---|---|---|
| Demo booked | Yes to a meeting | Booking link, creates the deal, drafts a confirmation |
| Interested | Positive, not ready | Mail with the card; call in 7 days |
| No answer | Nobody picked up | Retry in 2 working days at the other time of day; after the 4th try, nurture |
| Send information | They asked for something | Info mail; call in 7 days |
| Other outcome | Send email, Follow-up agreed, Bad timing (default 4 weeks), Not interested (with reason), Gatekeeper / Wrong person, Already contacted, Already a customer | Depends |
| Do not contact | They asked us to stop | Blocked forever in Twenty, Snov.io and Brevo |
Skip to (Tomorrow, 3 days, Next week, Next month, Skip 3 months) moves a task without logging. Never press No answer for a call you didn't make.

## The rules
1. Every touch goes through a button. No outreach from Gmail without a button press.
2. Log the no's too, with the reason.
3. One outcome per call or email.
4. Found a lead yourself: + Log activity, search first, then log.
5. A note after every real conversation: who, what they use today, what they care about.
6. Customers first check. ⚠️ Muligvis allerede kunde → if same owner: Other outcome → Already a customer.
7. One location per chain. Only contact the main company. 🔗 Chain called before → read history; if someone has them, Already contacted.
8. Do not contact is forever, on every channel.
9. **High-end, Michelin, and groups where we know the owner go to Alex:** Send to SDR → Alex with a short note. Don't email them yourself.
10. Another seller owns the company: don't contact; hand over or ask.
11. Demo booked is the only way a deal starts. HC or Alex run the demo with Maia.
12. Never delete records. Write a note and tell HC.

## What we say
1. Sell the meeting, not the product. Short.
2. Never "stempelkort" or "stamp card" as the headline. We do memberships, gift cards, prepaid, staff cards and more.
3. No discounts on restaurant demo cards.
4. No prices in the first contact. If pushed: "It starts at 179 kr a month, depending on what you need."
5. Social proof = customer names in their city and league, never percentages: Aamanns and Epicurus in Copenhagen; Jakobsen & Co, Nøgen and CARTER in Aarhus.
6. Never claim gift cards give marketing consent.
7. Never lie, not even small ("I was just walking past").
8. Options in writing: getregulars.com/proposal.
