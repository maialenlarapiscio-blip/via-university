# 01 Brand

## What Regulars is
Guest memberships in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet for hospitality brands. No app. No physical cards. Guests join in seconds, the venue knows who comes, how often and what they order, and can bring them back.

**Tagline:** Every Visit Matters.
**Positioning against competitors:** no app, your brand, your guests.

## Voice
1. Calm, direct, confident.
2. Outcome first, feature second.
3. Short sentences. No exclamation marks. No fake urgency.
4. "Guest" before signup, "member" after.
5. Say "memberships". Never "card" or "digital stamp card" as the main message.

## Languages
1. English and Danish.
2. Danish: each Danish line with the English translation under it; flag uncertain phrasing.

## Content themes (from the Brand Guidelines in Canva)
1. **New Regulars:** new clients and growth (the weekly partner welcome).
2. **Success Stories:** how clients use Regulars and what they achieve.
3. **Product & Integrations:** features, integrations (Workfeed, OnlinePOS), releases.
4. **Meet the Regulars:** stories about owners and guests.
5. **Local Insider Guide:** local hospitality recommendations.
6. **Insights:** perspective on hospitality, technology and guest relationships.

## Formats
1. Reels for reach. Carousels for saves.
2. On LinkedIn, posts by Alexander or team members beat page posts.

## Visual rules
1. **Background:** dark metallic. Near black #050505, to charcoal #0D0D0D, a restrained highlight #171717, back to near black.
2. **Display type:** Instrument Serif Italic, only for large headlines (not below about 32px digital or 24pt print). Expressive, not decorative.
3. **Body type:** Google Sans Flex. Regular for body, Medium for buttons and emphasis, Semibold for small labels. Avoid heavy weights.
4. **Writing:** sentence case by default. Uppercase only for short labels. Lines of 55 to 70 characters.
5. **Photography:** real hospitality photos, darkened so Regulars stands out (Alex: brightness about -80). [Add: what to avoid]
6. **Partner welcome post:** the Canva style of design DAHNSlQVpUA.
7. **Newsletter:** the fixed dark format Alex approved.
8. **Print kit:** Hans Christian's templates. Never redesign.
9. **Poster footer:** www.getregulars.com | info@getregulars.com | +45 31 34 55 85, muted grey, 14 to 16 px, centered.
