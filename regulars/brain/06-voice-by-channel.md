# 06 Voice by channel: email, LinkedIn, Instagram

Sources: Alexander's feedback in #gtm-marketing (Oct and Nov 2026), the Brand Guidelines in Canva, and Brevo results. If anything here contradicts a skill, this file wins.

## Core message (every channel)
1. **Tagline:** Every Visit Matters.
2. **What we do:** we help hospitality brands know their guests, build direct relationships and bring them back.
3. **How:** memberships in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet. No app. No physical cards.
4. **Outcome:** More guest data. More repeat visits. More revenue.
5. **Belief:** the relationship shouldn't end at payment. That's where it begins.
6. **Concrete version (Alex's edit):** It starts with one scan. Your guests join your membership, and from that moment you know who comes, how often and what they order.

## Word rules (Alex)
| Use | Avoid | Why |
|---|---|---|
| guest data, guest relationships | first party guest data (with clients) | Simpler, more natural |
| memberships | card, stamp card | Regulars is broader than a card |
| confident statement | question as headline | More authority |
| relevant across hospitality | only cafés, only Christmas | Every client should see themselves |
| no eyebrow | eyebrow by default | "Often just filling" |
| Signups (one word, noun) | Sign Ups | Labels need the noun |

Before writing anything, answer: **what should the reader do next, and why is that valuable for Regulars, our clients and their guests?**

## Instagram
1. **Audience:** owners and managers.
2. **Three pinned posts**, three separate posts, not a carousel: What ("We're Regulars"), Why ("Every Visit Matters. Great hospitality deserves lasting relationships."), How ("Turn every visit into the next.").
3. **Visual:** a nice hospitality photo, darkened (about -80 brightness) so Regulars stands out. Instrument Serif Italic headline. Near black ground.
4. **After the pinned posts:** real scenarios of how venues use Regulars, with partner permission if their membership is visible.
5. **CTA:** "Comment REGULARS" or a DM. One action per post.

## LinkedIn
1. **Audience:** owners, group CEOs, marketing leads.
2. **Format:** posts by Alexander or team members beat page posts. Carousels to teach.
3. **Tone:** industry perspective, short lines, one idea or one fact per post.
4. **One thread:** all drafts and FeedHive links go to the "LinkedIn posts" thread in #gtm-marketing.
5. **5W1H** with every proposal: Who, What, Why, Where, When, How.

## Email: client newsletter (Brevo, list "Regulars Newsletter")
1. **Audience:** current clients. Customer success, not a sales pitch.
2. **Goal:** clients use more of Regulars (switch on features, put the QR up, gift cards, prepaid).
3. **Sender:** HC from Regulars. Default CTA: reply to HC when there is no clear action. "Replying to HC is better than forcing a button."
4. **Format:** the fixed dark format. Short and easy to scan.
5. **Frequency:** to agree with HC and Alex. Alex thinks weekly "is a lot" because HC already talks to clients often.

## Email: outreach to new leads
**Where it happens: Twenty CRM (crm.getregulars.com), Call Center → Call mode.** If it isn't in Twenty, it didn't happen.
1. **Volume:** 10 outreach emails a day, 50 a week.
2. **How:** Twenty writes the draft with a demo card in the venue's own brand and books the follow-up call 7 days later. Maia adds **one personal line** and presses Send.
3. **Claude's job:** research the venue and write that one personal line (something only this venue has: its story, a dish, a recent opening, a press mention). Never rewrite Twenty's draft.
4. **All rules, the call script and objections:** see `11-outreach-playbook.md`. Social proof is customer names in their city, never percentages. No prices in first contact (if pushed: from 179 kr a month). High-end and Michelin go to Alex.

Before Twenty (still valid as style):
1. **Sequence:** first email plus one or two follow ups. Every follow up brings a new angle, never a repeat.
2. **Structure:** one personal line about the venue, what they gain, how it works in three steps, CTA for a short call.
3. **Alex's style:** remove fluff and repetition, value first, clear CTA.
4. **Test first:** send the sequence to Alex as if he were the client.
5. **Where to report:** #gtm-outbound.
6. **Subject:** simple, "{Venue} × Regulars".
