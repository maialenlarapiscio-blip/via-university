# 09 How Alex thinks

Alexander King approves Regulars marketing. This file sums up all of his feedback to Maia (Sep to Nov 2026: #gtm-marketing, its threads, and direct messages). Claude applies it **before** showing any draft, so it reaches Alex already aligned.

## 1. The five questions Alex always asks
Claude answers them in writing at the top of every proposal, before Alex has to ask:
1. **Who are we talking to?** Owners, marketing, operations or guests. One audience.
2. **What are we trying to win?** What should the reader do next?
3. **Why is it valuable?** For Regulars, for the client and for their guests. All three.
4. **What is the purpose, and how often?** He questions volume ("Why are we investing the time and sending a weekly message? It's a lot in my opinion.").
5. **Does it overlap with someone else?** ("Do you still feel this way after knowing that HC communicates so much with our clients at the same time?")

## 2. How Alex writes
1. **Short.** He removes fluff and repetition. Easy to scan.
2. **Value first, clear CTA.** One action.
3. **Confident statements, not questions.** "I prefer a confident statement over a question."
4. **Simple language.** "'Guest data' or 'guest relationships' feels more natural than 'first-party guest data'."
5. **No eyebrow** by default. "I think it's often just filling."
6. **Never "card".** "Don't use 'card'. Regulars is broader than that." Say "memberships".
7. **Relevant across hospitality.** Not only Christmas or January, and never "assuming guests only visit once".
8. **Exact grammar in labels.** "Signups" as one word when it is a noun.
9. **His brand lines:** Every Visit Matters. / No app. No physical cards. / More guest data. More repeat visits. More revenue. / The relationship shouldn't end at payment. That's where it begins. / Turn every visit into the next.
10. **Concrete beats abstract:** "you know who comes, how often and what they order".

## 3. How Alex gives visual feedback
1. **Exact measurements.** Poster example: wallet about 600 px and the widest element; headline about 500 px, centered; QR block about 500 px with a 30 px gap; 80 to 100 px between headline and wallet; 60 to 80 px between wallet and QR; badges centered with 20 px; everything on one vertical axis.
2. **Let Regulars stand out:** darken photos (about -80 brightness), check the logo size.
3. **Simplify:** remove bullet points, less text. Client posters: headline, phone mockup, QR, a subtle dark grey "Powered by Regulars" at the bottom.
4. **Standard footer:** www.getregulars.com | info@getregulars.com | +45 31 34 55 85, muted grey, 14 to 16 px, centered.
5. He says "these are suggestions, adjust for the best visual balance": he wants judgement, not a literal copy.

## 4. How Alex works with the team
1. **One thread per topic** in #gtm-marketing: LinkedIn posts, Instagram (Pinned Posts), Newsletters, Email outreach. Always reply inside the thread (he once deleted a reply posted in the channel).
2. **Sales in #gtm-outbound**, deals in #gtm-deals, new clients in #gtm-csm.
3. **Test before sending:** he wants to receive emails first "as if I was a potential client".
4. **Sequences, not single emails:** first message and follow ups, each with a new angle.
5. **He proposes, he does not impose:** he ends with "What do you think?". He expects Maia to answer with a view and reasons, and likes it when she does.
6. **Tables for planning:** one row per item, with date, topic and goal.
7. **A 30 minute weekly touchpoint** to align, share updates and flag anything that needs support.
8. **Don't jump ahead:** "Set a reminder for sometime in 14 days and don't focus on Christmas yet."
9. **Keep client follow ups human and simple:** "Hope you're good! How's everything so far?" scheduled for early in the morning.

## 5. Checklist before anything goes to Alex
1. Does it say who it is for and what action we want?
2. Does it say why it is valuable for Regulars, the client and the guest?
3. Is it short, with no eyebrow, no "card", no question headline?
4. Does the visual let Regulars stand out and respect the measurements?
5. Is it going to the right thread, with 5W1H and the FeedHive or Canva link?
6. Does it end with one concrete question so Alex can decide fast?
