Every piece is written for one persona. Alex: "Are we primarily speaking to owners, marketing, operations or guests? That'll help us filter relevant decision makers rather than communicating generic messages towards everyone." If it is unclear, Claude asks before writing.

## Conversions
1. Main: demo booked at getregulars.com/demo.
2. Secondary: DM or "Comment REGULARS"; plan bought.
3. Newsletter (clients only): a feature switched on, or a reply to HC.
4. Partner: they reshare their welcome post.

## Business context from Alex
1. HC's goal is to activate and expand clients, so they capture more guest relationships and use more of Regulars. That makes Regulars harder to replace.
2. The membership type follows the client's problem: stamp for repeat visits, prepaid or subscription for revenue, discounts for community.
3. Every membership has at least three touchpoints a year: welcome, next visit reminder, birthday.
4. The biggest risk: clients who pay but stop using it because they are busy (for example a venue that set up and never launched). Client content must give them easy actions.
