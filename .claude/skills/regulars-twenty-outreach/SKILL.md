# Regulars Twenty outreach

Source of truth: `regulars/brain/11-outreach-playbook.md`. Read it first. This skill does the research and writing so Maia's 75 minute email block takes about 30. **It never sends, never presses Twenty buttons and never edits Twenty's email body.** Maia presses the buttons and Send.

## Input
Maia pastes, for each company: the name, and whatever the Call mode brief shows (city, website, contact, any ⚠️ or 🔗 flags, Tidligere kontakt). A list of names and cities also works.

## For each company, in order
1. **Stop check (rules 6 to 10).** Flag and do not write a first line if:
   1. the brief says ⚠️ Muligvis allerede kunde → "Check owner; if same: Other outcome → Already a customer";
   2. 🔗 Chain called before or earlier contact under Tidligere kontakt → "Read history; maybe Already contacted";
   3. the venue is high-end, Michelin, or a group where we know the owner → "Send to SDR → Alex, with this note: …" (write the note);
   4. Do not contact, or another seller owns it → "Skip".
2. **Research (2 to 3 minutes' worth):** website, Instagram, booking button. Find what they sell, who owns it, which booking or loyalty system they use, and anything recent (new opening, menu, press). Use only what you actually found; say "not found" otherwise. Never invent.
3. **Contact:** owner or manager by name if you found one; otherwise say "use info@, look for the owner".
4. **Language:** Danish venue → DA, unless the owner clearly writes English.
5. **Note for this call** (for Twenty): one or two lines, the fact plus the system they use.
6. **First line** for the Gmail draft, in the chosen language: one real fact, warm, short, no flattery, no lie, no "card" as headline, no price, no discount. Like the playbook examples: "Saw you just opened a second shop on Jægersborggade, congratulations." Give the Danish line with the English translation under it, and flag uncertain Danish.
7. **Demo card type** Twenty will use, for Maia to check: café = stamp, restaurant or bakery = cashback, bar or hotel = membership.

## Output
1. A short summary: how many ready, how many stopped and why.
2. A table, one row per company: Company · Stop? · Contact · Lang · Note for this call · First line · Card.
3. Then the stopped ones with the exact button or Alex note.

## Follow-up call prep (when Maia asks)
For each call due: the name, what was in the note, one opening line with [Brand], the likely objection and the playbook answer, and the two-option close. Use the social proof names from the playbook only for the matching city (Aamanns, Epicurus in Copenhagen; Jakobsen & Co, Nøgen, CARTER in Aarhus).

## End of day
Remind Maia of the daily checklist: replies answered, 10 Send email logged, 10 drafts sent, due calls logged, Overdue empty, notes saved.
