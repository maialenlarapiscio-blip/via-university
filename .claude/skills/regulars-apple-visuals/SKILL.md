# Regulars Apple visuals

Rules from Hans Christian (Regulars, Slack DM, 16 Oct 2026), checked against Apple's official guidelines. Apply them to every Regulars design that contains an Apple device, Apple UI, the Wallet name or the Add to Apple Wallet badge (Instagram and LinkedIn posts, ads, roll-ups, posters, newsletters, videos, web). Run the checklist at the end before showing any design to Maia.

## Why these rules exist
Regulars may use Apple artwork because it is a member of the Apple Developer Program and accepted the Apple Wallet Marketing Artwork License. That license covers marketing of Regulars passes only, and it ends automatically if the rules are broken. Regulars is NOT an Apple partner. Never write or imply "partnership with Apple", "Apple approved", "endorsed by Apple" or similar, in copy, captions, hashtags or alt text.

## 1. Scope
1. Use Apple device images only around Regulars' own software: Regulars Wallet passes (including partner cards built on Regulars, such as Breadhead), Regulars web pages, Regulars app screens. Never as decoration or around anything that is not ours.
2. The license covers our passes only.

## 2. Device frames (default device: iPhone 18 Pro)
3. Use only the official product bezels from developer.apple.com/design/resources, latest generation (currently iPhone 18 Pro; Pro Max and iPhone Duo also available). Never draw, illustrate, 3D render, AI generate or otherwise simulate an iPhone. Exception: simple illustrations in instructional material (how to guides, tutorial videos). Generic device illustrations must not show Apple specific details (Dynamic Island, camera layout, buttons).
4. Use bezels exactly as delivered: no shadows, reflections, highlights, glows, tilting, perspective, cropping, flipping, rotating, spinning, animating, cases, or anything covering the device. Nothing may enter or break out of the screen.
5. Text, labels, stickers and violators go beside the device, never on top of it. In the Regulars Canva style (photo in front of the big "Regulars" text), the phone must stay fully visible and uncovered.
6. Minimum device size: 25 mm tall in print, 200 px tall on screen, and always clearly legible. Keep correct relative scale if several Apple products appear.
7. No competing devices (Android phones, Samsung, etc.) in the same image. Regulars cards also work in Google Wallet: mention that only in copy, never by showing an Android phone next to an iPhone.
8. Never turn a device image into a button, icon, logo or die cut shape.
Note: the Regulars design system README says phone mockups get "realistic shadows and cinematic light". Apple rule 4 wins: no shadow on the bezel. Flag this to Maia if it comes up.

## 3. Making it look realistic (the compliant way)
Realistic means: official flat bezel, straight on, with real Regulars UI on the latest iOS and the official iOS 27 status bar (9:41, full signal, full Wi Fi, full battery). It does NOT mean adding depth, shadows or angles to the bezel.
For a more lifestyle, real world look, use custom photography or video instead: a real person holding a real iPhone with a real Regulars pass on screen. Rules for photos and video: straight on shots preferred, no extreme angles, device color and shape accurate, no cases, no fantasy or impossible scenes, do not feature or hide the Apple logo, no Android device in the shot. In video, show the whole device first, then simple pan or zoom, fade or dissolve transitions only, no Apple gestures as transitions, no native Apple sounds, start with the app open, add "sequences shortened" if shortened.

## 5. Recipe: pass inside an iPhone 18 Pro (portrait)
1. Bezel PNG: 1350 x 2760 px, transparent screen. Screen opening bbox: x 72 to 1278, y 69 to 2691 (1206 x 2622 px = 402 x 874 pt at 3x). Same for all 4 colors.
2. Build the screen at 402 x 874 CSS px and render at device scale 3 (1206 x 2622): black or Wallet background, status bar SVG at top 0 (402 x 62), the pass from about y 118 with 18 px side margins, pass corners rounded about 12 px. Use real Regulars pass screenshots (crop away any white border) or a faithful rebuild of the real pass.
3. Composite: flood fill the transparent region from the screen center to get the screen mask, dilate it a few px, paste the screen under the mask, then alpha composite the untouched bezel on top. Never alter bezel pixels.
4. Place the phone PNG in the layout straight on, no shadow, text beside it.
5. Resolution: a pasted screenshot under about 1100 px wide looks soft; prefer a native iPhone screenshot or a Pass Designer preview. For print (roll-ups, posters) the 2760 px bezel gives about 115 dpi at 60 cm tall: fine at viewing distance, tell Maia.

## 6. Screen content
9. The screen shows real or realistic Regulars UI (a Regulars pass in Wallet, our web page, our app). Build iOS system UI (status bar, Wallet, Lock Screen) from Apple's official iOS 27 UI Kit, never hand drawn copies. Never show a blank screen.
10. Status bar: 9:41, full signal, full Wi Fi, full battery. Use a localized layer that matches the market.
11. Use fictional guest data (names, points, numbers), never a real person's data. Partner venue names and logos (e.g. Breadhead) only with the partner's OK: remind Maia before publishing.
12. Push notifications: show one single Regulars notification on the Lock Screen. No other apps' notifications, no Home Screen.
13. Never use icons, logos or images taken from apple.com, and never use the standalone Apple logo or the Wallet icon alone.

## 7. Add to Apple Wallet badge
14. Only Apple's official badge files: SVG for web and email, EPS for print. Use the localized version (Danish for Danish material). Never recreate, recolor, rotate, flip, animate, dim, or add shadows, glows or reflections.
15. Clear space: at least 0.1 x badge height on screen. Nothing may overlap it.
16. Background: white or light. On very dark backgrounds use Apple's outline version.
17. The badge is always secondary to the main message and to Regulars or the partner brand. Never the main graphic.
18. Print: badge next to the QR code, ideally the same width, no taller than 25 mm (per Hans and the Wallet guidelines PDF; confirm in the current PDF for big print runs).
19. The badge must lead to a real, signed pass. In email and web, place it near the pass and add a line like: "To add this card to Wallet, open this email on your iPhone."

## 8. Naming and copy
20. Write exactly: iPhone (lowercase i even at the start of a sentence, never IPHONE), Apple Wallet (first mention), then Wallet. Never translate these names, also in Danish copy. Never say "smartphone" for an iPhone.
21. Copy focuses on Regulars and the guest benefit, not on Apple features. Use referential phrasing: "works with Apple Wallet", "Regulars card for iPhone".
22. Never imitate Apple's own ads or communications style closely enough to look like Apple made it.
23. Where legal text exists (website footer, newsletter footer, long brochures), add the credit line listing only the marks used, e.g.: "Apple, iPhone and Apple Wallet are trademarks of Apple Inc., registered in the U.S. and other countries." Trademark symbols (®) only in US only material.

## When unsure
Leave the Apple element out and flag it to Maia so she can ask Hans. For big items (paid ads, campaigns, packaging, print runs) remind Maia to have Hans or Alexander check the current Apple license PDFs.

## Pre delivery checklist
Before showing any design, confirm and report in one short line per failed point:
1. Is the Apple element around Regulars software only?
2. Official iPhone 18 Pro bezel (or real photo), untouched, straight on, uncovered, no shadow?
3. Text beside the device, not on it? Device at least 200 px or 25 mm tall?
4. No Android or other brand device in the image?
5. Screen: real Regulars UI, iOS 27 status bar (9:41, full signal, Wi Fi, battery), fictional data, partner OK, not blank?
6. Badge (if any): official localized file, light background or outline version, clear space, secondary, links to a real pass?
7. Copy: iPhone and Apple Wallet spelled right, no partnership or endorsement claim, no "smartphone"?
8. Credit line added where there is legal text? Resolution fine for the format?
