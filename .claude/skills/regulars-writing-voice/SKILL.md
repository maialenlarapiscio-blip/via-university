# Regulars writing voice

The examples below matter more than the rules. Match their rhythm, length and word choice. Then run the checklist in `regulars/brain/09-how-alex-thinks.md`.

## Rules
1. Short lines. One idea per line. Plenty of white space.
2. Outcome first: what the venue gets, then how.
3. Confident statements. No question headlines, no exclamation marks, no urgency.
4. Say "memberships", "guests", "guest data", "guest relationships". Never "card", "loyalty app", "first party guest data" (with clients).
5. Concrete over abstract: "you know who comes, how often and what they order".
6. End with one action.
7. No eyebrow, no filler intros, no hashtags walls.

## Words we use
Every Visit Matters · memberships · Apple Wallet and Google Wallet · No app. No physical cards. · guests · regulars · bring them back · repeat visits · direct relationships

## Words we avoid
card · stamp card (as the headline idea) · loyalty program · app · revolutionize · unlock · game changer · exclamation marks

## Example 1: Instagram pinned post "What" (Alex, approved direction)
> We're Regulars.
>
> We help hospitality brands collect more guest data, build stronger relationships, and generate more revenue from existing guests.
>
> Powered by memberships in Apple & Google Wallet.
>
> No apps. No physical cards.

Alex's sharper version of the body:
> It starts with one scan. Your guests join your membership, and from that moment you know who comes, how often and what they order.
>
> All powered by memberships in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet. No app. No physical cards.

## Example 2: Instagram pinned post "Why" (Alex)
> Every Visit Matters.
> Great hospitality deserves lasting relationships.
>
> Most hospitality brands know what they sell, but not who's buying.
>
> Once guests pay and leave, the relationship often ends.
>
> We believe it shouldn't.
>
> That's why we're building Regulars. To help hospitality brands know their guests, build direct relationships, and bring them back.
>
> Because the relationship shouldn't end at payment. That's where it begins.

## Example 3: Client newsletter brief (HC, sent as Prepaid, 58.5% open rate)
> Headline: Get paid for the next five coffees today
>
> Lead: Your regulars pay upfront for a bundle of coffees, and the membership lives in their Apple or Google Wallet.
>
> Body: Guests pay for 4 coffees and get 5. Staff scan it at the till, and one coffee is used. When the last one is gone, auto refill can top it up on its own. Guests already on your stamp membership can move straight to prepaid.
>
> Button: See how prepaid works

## Example 4: How it works, in steps (Alex)
> Turn every visit into the next.
>
> 1. Guests join your membership in seconds.
> 2. Their membership lives in Apple & Google Wallet.
> 3. You collect and unify guest data.
> 4. You engage guests with personalized messages, offers, and rewards.
> 5. Guests return, spend more, and build stronger relationships.
>
> More guest data. More repeat visits. More revenue.

## Outreach email shape (Alex's rules, Nov 2026)
1. Subject: "{Venue} × Regulars".
2. One personal line that proves you looked at them.
3. What they gain, in one sentence.
4. How it works in three short steps.
5. One question for a short call.
6. Follow up: a new angle (a feature, a similar venue, a seasonal moment), never "just checking in" and never a repeat.
